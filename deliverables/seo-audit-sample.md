## 1. Technical SEO

### Issues Found
| Issue | Severity | Impact |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Missing meta descriptions | High | -12% CTR |
| Slow LCP (4.2s) | High | -8% ranking |
| No sitemap.xml | Medium | Indexing delay |
| Broken internal links (12) | Medium | Lost link equity |

### Fixes
1. Add meta descriptions to all pages (template: `{Primary Keyword} | {Brand}`)
2. Optimize images (WebP format, lazy loading) → target LCP < 2.5s
3. Generate and submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console
4. Fix 404 links via redirect mapping

**Effort**: 2 days | **Expected gain**: +34% indexed pages
